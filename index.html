About me

Education first. Technology with purpose.

My professional background is rooted in international education and academic coordination, where I work directly with curriculum, assessment, teachers and educational data.

That experience increasingly led me towards software development, automation, data analysis and artificial intelligence — not as isolated technologies, but as tools for solving real educational problems.

Today, I combine academic leadership with the development and exploration of digital solutions that can make school processes clearer, faster and more useful.
